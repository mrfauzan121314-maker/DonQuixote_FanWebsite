Hunters of the City

The streets of the City are never short of vile and savage company after dark. Rats desperate to turn their lives around, denizens of the Backstreets harboring dreams of revenge, Syndicates and Fingers embroiled in struggles over their interests… and, of course, the Sweepers. For all the fortunes to be made from working after sundown, few Fixers are eager to accept such contracts. There are, however, those who find their greatest opportunities precisely where others dare not tread. The discernment to read any situation with remarkable precision. The swiftness and thoroughness to bring a job to its conclusion without inviting further trouble. With these two qualities as its greatest assets, Wedge Office has weathered every contract and walked away with the handsome compensation that follows.
